Attributes

Players have 6 attributes (Offense, Defense, Speed, Power, Stamina, Technique). Each position weighs them differently; the combined value is "OVR".

  • Each attribute has a cap it can't grow beyond. Caps can be raised by camps and talent awakening.
  • Each skill adds +1 to OVR; a unique skill adds +3.
  • Other clubs' players' attributes become more accurate the more your scouts check them. Unscouted players show ranges.

Fatigue and form

  • Fatigue goes from 0 to 100. More fatigue lowers training effect (up to −10%) and match performance (up to −20%).
  • Fatigue recovers by 18 every week. Players rested individually (up to 3) recover 35.
  • Playing 90 minutes adds about 24 fatigue (less for high-stamina players).
  • Training with fatigue over 70 has a 30% chance to lower form.
  • Form (5 levels, Terrible to Excellent) changes growth by −10% to +10% and match strength by −6% to +6%.
  • Form tends to rise after a win (30%) and drop after a loss (25%), and always rises for the MVP. Rest helps (45%, 65% for individual rest), but team rest and training only raise form up to Good, and individual rest only up to Normal. Use a pre-match team talk to lift players to Good. 3+ weeks on the bench can lower it.
  • Excellent form comes only from a win or an MVP. Excellent and Terrible form don't last: each week there is a 30% chance they move one step back toward normal (Good / Poor).

Playing also gives EXP (30 per 90 minutes, +30 for MVP). Giving young players minutes helps them grow.

Match factors

The Zone

Two successes within 6 minutes (beating a player, winning a tackle, a save or a goal) put a player in "the Zone": +8 to attributes for 10 minutes. Interceptions don't count.

Tricks

Scissors, Marseille roulette, Cruyff turn and heel lift. Signature tricks come out more often and fool defenders more easily. Up to 2 per player.

  • Players with Technique 50+ doing technique training occasionally learn a new trick (about 2% a week at 60, 3% at 80).
  • The heel lift needs Technique 65.
  • More technical players are more likely to start with a trick (about 10% at 50, 50% at 70).

Ace Duel

The ace is the starting attacker (ST, CF, WG, OMF, SMF) with the best Offense, Technique and Speed. The defender opposite them (CB, SB, DMF) is the "defensive key".

The pairing is shown before the match, so put a strong defender opposite the opposing ace.

Fatigue & subs

Fatigue also builds during matches and slows players down. Sub low-stamina players in the second half, or use players with "Iron man" or "Inexhaustible", to avoid late goals.

Talent awakening

Very rarely, one of your players awakens. Each of the 6 attribute caps rises by +5 to 12, and current attributes by +3 to 6 (once per player).

  • More likely with a higher training facility level (×1 at level 1, ×2.6 at level 5).
  • More likely for players with lower caps (a comeback chance for limited players).
  • Most likely during the growth period (from starting to grow until the peak, ×3); almost never after decline begins.
  • Even in the best conditions it's about 5% over a career. Developing youth graduates for a long time gives you more chances.

Youth

  • Candidates are 18. There are 3, plus 1 per youth dormitory level (up to 6).
  • Youth Recollection (looking back on ages 15–18) is once a year. Youth dormitory and youth training ground at level 3+ each add one more (up to 3).
  • The youth training ground also improves growth in recollection (+5% per level).

Money

  • You start with ¥124.5M and an 8,000-seat stadium.
  • Income: sponsors (signed only in July), ticket income (home matches; more popularity and supporters bring more fans) and prize money.
  • League prize for 1st place: ¥200M in Division 1, ¥140M in Division 2, ¥80M in Division 3. It falls with position; last place gets 20% of the winner's.
  • Popularity: +0.6 per win, +0.1 per draw, −0.4 per loss; +4 for a title and +6 for promotion.
  • Below ¥10M is a warning; below −¥30M is a crisis. Two loss-making seasons in a row bring a penalty.

Transfers

  • Transfers are only possible in summer (Jul Wk 1 – Sep Wk 2) and winter (Feb Wk 1 – Mar Wk 1).
  • Squads must have 18–30 players.
  • Having scouts check a player reveals accurate attributes and skills. Sign without scouting and they may be weaker than you hoped.
  • Popular clubs in higher divisions attract better players. Winning in Asia and the world raises your reputation further.

The road to the top

  1. Get promoted from Division 3. 18 clubs per league; the top 3 go up and the bottom 3 go down (3 points for a win, 1 for a draw).
  2. Finish in the top 3 of Division 1, or win the domestic cup. You qualify for next season's Asian tournament (4 clubs from your country).
  3. Win the Asian tournament (July–August). Prize money grows each round; the winner gets ¥80M.
  4. Win the Club World Cup (December). 16 clubs compete; the winner's prize is ¥200M. World champions clears the game (you can keep playing).

Tactic cards and team talks

  • The pre-match talk can be given once per match. Its effect depends on the strength gap (3 or more means stronger or weaker).
TalkStronger opponentEvenWeaker opponent
Encourage60% form up40%20%
Get toughUp 15% / down 25%Up 30% / down 10%Up 50%
Calm them down15% up; 55% in cup and rival matches
  • Talks raise form only up to Good. Players aged 21 or under are more likely to drop when you get tough (+15%).
  • Half-time talk (3D matches): Get tough when losing, Encourage when level, Calm them down when winning, for up to +4% in the second half. Calming the team while losing costs 2%.
  • Tactic cards (3D matches): up to 3 per match, each lasting 15 minutes. All-out attack pushes players forward, Hold the lead drops the line, and Attack the flanks uses the width of the pitch.

Rivals and season objectives

  • Your rival is one club in your league (last season's rival if still there, otherwise the closest in strength). Rival matches draw 1.3x the crowd; a win gives +2 popularity, a loss −1, and results also move your players' form.
  • Each season has 3 objectives: league position, a cup run (or goals if you're not in the cup), and a supporters' wish (home wins, youth appearances or goals).

Friends Cup and memory cards

  • In Team Data Exchange → Friends Cup, play a knockout tournament against up to 7 registered friends' teams. Missing slots become first-round byes. Draws go to extra time, then penalties.
  • The Friends Cup is a friendly: it doesn't affect growth, fatigue, records or money.
  • Make a "memory card" from the match result screen or the cup result. On PC it's saved to Pictures\MiracleSoccer; on phones (Android 10+) to your photos, and Share sends it to social media or messaging apps.

Achievements (23)

Matches

  • First win: win your first official match
  • 5 / 10 wins in a row: in official matches
  • Goal rush: score 5+ goals in one match
  • Hat-trick: one player scores 3+ in a match
  • Unbeaten season: finish the league without losing

Club

  • Division 3 title, To Division 2, To Division 1, Division 1 title
  • Domestic cup winners, Asian champions, World champions
  • Rule the world: win the Club World Cup 3 times

Play

  • Goal from a trick: beat a player with a trick and score
  • Heel lift and score
  • Zone strike: a player in the Zone scores
  • Ace Duel win: your ace beats the defensive key
  • Shut down the ace: stop the opposing ace 2+ times without being beaten

Players & club

  • Technician: a player with 2 tricks
  • Youth star: a youth graduate scores in an official match
  • Talent awakening: a player's talent awakens
  • Wealthy club: funds exceed ¥1B
